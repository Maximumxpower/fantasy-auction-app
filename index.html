Welcome to Draft Assistant Pro

Your ultimate real-time fantasy football auction war room

Draft Assistant Pro gives you a real-time mathematical edge during auction drafts by dynamically recalculating your open roster targets after every dollar spent.

📊

1. Header & Live Metrics

• Max Bid Allowed: The exact ceiling you can bid while keeping $1 for every remaining open slot.
• Dynamic Target Adj: Re-allocates remaining budget across open slots automatically as you hit bargains or reaches.
• Display Toggle: Switch between $ USD and % Budget for custom league budgets.

📋

2. Bulk Importer & Auto-Tiers

• Instant Import: Copy/paste whole cheat sheets directly from FantasyPros or ESPN.
• Position Tiers: Auto-groups players into positional tiers using VORP cliff detection.
• Interactive Queue: Drag & drop player badges straight into your Nomination Queue.

📋

3. Live Roster & Draft Recap

• Inline Table Editing: Click slot names or target plans directly in the table to edit mid-draft.
• Variance Tracking: Highlights bargains (green) and overpays (red) instantly.
• Recap Board: Analyzes net drafting value, efficiency scores, and biggest bargains.

📈

4. Room Sales & Market Trends

• Log Room Sales: Record competitor picks to track overall league spending.
• Room Inflation Index: Live gauge indicating whether managers are overspending or leaving market value on the table.

💡
Pro Tip Ticker: Check the scrolling ticker at the bottom of the app for real-time tactical auction strategies!
