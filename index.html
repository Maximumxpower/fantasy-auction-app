How to use this app during the draft

🎯 Targets Tab

Players sorted by auction value, grouped into position tiers. Drag any player card into your Nomination Queue. NFL team colors let you spot players at a glance.

⚡ Draft Tab — Log Picks

Type the player name and final price, hit Log Pick. Your Max Bid and per-position adjusted targets recalculate automatically after every entry.

📊 Room Tab — Market Intel

Log what opponents are paying. The Inflation Index tells you if the room is running hot — an overpaying room makes your remaining budget more powerful.

🏆 Recap Tab — Draft Grade

After the draft, get your letter grade, net value vs. spend, biggest bargains, and biggest reaches — shareable as plain text.

⚖️ Scale Values to Roster

Works for any league format — 8-team, 14-team, superflex, TEP, dynasty. After importing, hit Scale Values to Roster and the math reshapes around your exact slot count and positional scarcity. RB-heavy roster? RB values get a scarcity premium. Deep QB pool? QB dollars spread wider.

💡 Suggest Plans — Smart Budget Allocation

Hit Suggest Plans and the app reads your remaining budget, your open roster slots, and the actual values of players still available at each position — then calculates exactly how many dollars each slot deserves. Mid-draft it adapts: as you win players and spend money, run it again and the remaining targets reproportion automatically. No spreadsheet math, no guessing.
