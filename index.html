How to use this app during the draft

🎯 Targets Tab

Players sorted by auction value, grouped into position tiers. Drag any player card into your Nomination Queue. NFL team colors let you spot players at a glance.

⚡ Draft Tab — Log Picks

Type the player name and final price, hit Log Pick. Your Max Bid and per-position adjusted targets recalculate automatically after every entry.

📊 Room Tab — Market Intel

Log what opponents are paying. The Inflation Index tells you if the room is running hot — an overpaying room makes your remaining budget more powerful.

🏆 Recap Tab — Draft Grade

After the draft, get your letter grade, net value vs. spend, biggest bargains, and biggest reaches — shareable as plain text.
